# Facts — Hadean Eon

*General facts about the Hadean eon (~4.6–4.0 Gya), for any chapter to reference. Not chapter-specific — chapter factsheets should point here rather than duplicating.*

- Earth accreted starting ~4.5 billion years ago. (established — radiometric dating)
- Heavy bombardment by planetesimals/asteroids during early accretion and differentiation lasted on the order of hundreds of millions of years. (established broadly — though the "Late Heavy Bombardment" as a discrete later spike ~3.9 Gya is a separate, debated hypothesis, distinct from ongoing early accretion)
- The Moon formed via a giant impact roughly contemporaneously with early Earth (~4.5 Gya) and was initially much closer than its current distance, receding gradually via tidal interaction ever since. (established mechanism and trend; the exact early distance/apparent size in the sky is modeled, not directly observed)
- The young Sun was fainter than today — the "faint young Sun" is a well-established result of stellar evolution models, with the early Sun at roughly 70–75% of its current luminosity. (established)
- Earth's earliest atmosphere lacked free oxygen and would not have supported human respiration; likely dominated by CO₂, N₂, some CH₄/H₂, plus volcanic gases. (established — no free O₂ existed until much later, after oxygenic photosynthesis)
- Liquid-water oceans are inferred to have existed by ~4.4–4.3 Gya, based on zircon mineral evidence, implying relatively rapid cooling and condensation of early steam/water vapor. (plausible — indirect mineralogical evidence, not direct observation)
