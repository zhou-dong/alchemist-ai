# Facts — Archean Eon

*General facts about the Archean eon (~4.0–2.5 Gya), for any chapter to reference. Not chapter-specific — chapter factsheets should point here rather than duplicating.*

- Begins after oceans and a stable crust are already in place, following the Hadean. (established — standard geologic timescale definition)
- Atmosphere still lacked free oxygen; O₂ did not become significant until the Great Oxidation Event, ~2.4 Gya, near the end of the Archean/start of the Proterozoic. (established)
- A hazy, orange/pink-tinted sky from photochemical hydrocarbon haze (organic haze from methane photochemistry) has been proposed for parts of the Archean atmosphere. (plausible — a specific, debated hypothesis, e.g. Sagan & Chyba's "pale orange dot"; haze intensity is sensitive to the assumed CH₄/CO₂ ratio)
- The young Sun remained fainter than today through the Archean, gradually brightening. (established — stellar evolution models)
